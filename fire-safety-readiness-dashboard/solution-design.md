# Solution Design

## Workflow

1. Maintain fire and safety equipment register in Power Apps.
2. Track inspection frequency and next due date.
3. UiPath sends reminders for upcoming checks.
4. Fluid AI answers readiness questions.
5. Reports are generated for managers and audits.

## Readiness Score

Readiness score can be calculated from:

- Percentage of assets inspected on time
- Number of high-risk overdue checks
- Emergency drill completion
- Critical equipment availability
