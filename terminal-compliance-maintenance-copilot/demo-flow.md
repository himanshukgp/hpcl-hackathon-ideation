# Demo Flow

## 5 Minute Hackathon Demo

1. Open Power Apps dashboard showing all terminal compliance tasks.
2. Import a sample Excel task schedule using UiPath.
3. Show new tasks appearing with due dates and owners.
4. Filter to "Due This Week" and "Overdue".
5. Trigger UiPath reminder workflow.
6. Show email or Teams reminder sent to task owner.
7. Mark one task complete in Power Apps and attach sample evidence.
8. Ask Fluid AI: "Summarize current compliance status."
9. Ask Fluid AI: "Which overdue items are high risk?"
10. Show weekly report generated by UiPath.

## Demo Dataset

Use 15-20 sample tasks across safety, maintenance, statutory, operations, and audit categories.

## Demo Highlight

The strongest moment is the shift from manual tracking to an automated command center where a manager can ask questions instead of searching through files.
