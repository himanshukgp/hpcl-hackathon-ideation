# Problem Statement

HPCL terminals have many periodic operational, maintenance, safety, and compliance activities with different due dates and owners. Missing a task can create safety risk, audit observations, operational delays, or compliance gaps.

## Current Pain Points

- Recurring tasks are scattered across Excel sheets, registers, email, and individual memory.
- Supervisors have to manually follow up with multiple teams.
- Overdue tasks may be noticed late, often close to audit or inspection dates.
- Evidence such as photos, certificates, calibration reports, and checklists is hard to retrieve.
- Management does not get a real-time view of terminal readiness.

## Users

- Terminal operations team
- Maintenance team
- Safety officer
- Terminal manager
- Compliance or audit team

## Success Criteria

- Every recurring task has an owner, frequency, due date, and status.
- Users get reminders before due dates.
- Overdue tasks are escalated automatically.
- Completion evidence is stored against the task.
- Managers can ask AI for current compliance status.
