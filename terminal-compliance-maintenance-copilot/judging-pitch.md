# Judging Pitch

## One-Line Pitch

An AI-powered compliance command center that prevents missed terminal maintenance and safety deadlines.

## Why It Matters

Every missed inspection, calibration, or statutory task can create safety exposure, audit observations, and operational risk. The solution reduces dependency on manual follow-up and makes terminal readiness visible in real time.

## Innovation

- Combines low-code task tracking, RPA reminders, and conversational AI.
- Converts scattered Excel/email/register workflows into an auditable digital process.
- Lets managers ask operational questions directly instead of searching multiple files.

## Business Impact

- Fewer missed compliance deadlines
- Reduced manual follow-up effort
- Better audit preparedness
- Improved visibility for terminal managers
- Faster escalation of high-risk overdue items

## Feasibility

The prototype can be built with Power Apps, UiPath, Fluid AI, Excel, and SharePoint/OneDrive without deep system integration.
