# Problem Statement

Permit-to-work processes are safety-critical but can become slow and paperwork-heavy. Missing checklist items, delayed approvals, or poor closure records can create operational and safety risk.

## Pain Points

- Permit forms may be incomplete.
- Approval status is hard to track.
- Safety checklist verification depends on manual review.
- Closure evidence may not be attached consistently.
- Supervisors spend time chasing approvers.

## Success Criteria

- Permit request captures all required information.
- Approval chain is visible.
- Missing information is flagged before submission.
- Permit PDF is generated automatically.
- Closure evidence and remarks are stored.
