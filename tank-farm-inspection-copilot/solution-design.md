# Solution Design

## Workflow

1. Inspector selects tank or area in Power Apps.
2. Checklist items are completed with pass/fail status.
3. Defect photos and remarks are uploaded.
4. UiPath creates defect tickets and sends alerts.
5. Fluid AI summarizes defects by severity and location.

## Inspection Categories

- Tank shell and roof
- Valves and pipelines
- Bund wall and drains
- Firefighting equipment
- Safety signage
- Housekeeping
