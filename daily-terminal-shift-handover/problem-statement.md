# Problem Statement

Shift handovers often depend on informal notes, calls, messages, or registers. Important pending actions may be missed when teams change shifts.

## Pain Points

- Handover details are inconsistent.
- Incoming shift may miss abnormal observations.
- Pending tasks are not always tracked.
- Supervisors lack a consolidated view.

## Success Criteria

- Every shift records structured handover details.
- Pending actions are extracted and assigned.
- Incoming shift sees critical alerts first.
- Daily summaries are automatically shared.
